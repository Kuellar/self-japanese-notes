    Notes

    • The Dictionary Form is the basic form of the verb and is also the Plain Present Affirmative form.
    • The Formal Present Affirmative form uses ます.
    • The Formal Present Negative form uses ません.
    • The Formal Past Affirmative form uses ました.
    • The Formal Past Negative form uses ませんでした.
    • The Plain Present Negative form uses the appropriate ない conjugation.
    • The Plain Past Affirmative and Plain Past Negative forms follow the conjugation rules of each verb group.
    • 有る is the verb ある. Its negative is conventionally 無い rather than 有らない.
